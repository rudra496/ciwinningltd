About CI WINNING LTD

Winning futures through global education — honest guidance, personal support and proven processes for students across the world, from the UK and Nigeria to Bangladesh and beyond.

Our Story

Built for Students, Driven by Results

CI WINNING LTD is a UK-registered educational support services company. We exist for one reason: to help students win places at universities where they can genuinely thrive.

Applying to study abroad is complex — entry requirements, English tests, documents, deadlines, visas, accommodation. Many students lose money and time on applications that were never right for their profile in the first place. We change that with honest assessment first, careful shortlisting, and hands-on support through every submission.

Our team supports undergraduate and postgraduate applicants into the United Kingdom — our flagship destination — as well as Canada, the Netherlands and other countries on request. And our support doesn't end at acceptance: we prepare you for departure and stay with you as you settle into student life.

We provide student-focused admissions support while working with established education recruitment organisations and authorised partners.

  • Assessment before application — we only recommend paths where your profile is genuinely competitive.
  • Full-journey coverage — nine services from first consultation to arrival orientation.
  • Reachable when it matters — support across UK and West African time zones, in person and online.
