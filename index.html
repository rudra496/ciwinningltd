FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for the initial consultation?
No. Your first consultation and eligibility assessment are completely free — in person, by phone or on WhatsApp. Paid work begins only if you decide to proceed with an application through us.
Which countries can you help me study in?
The United Kingdom is our flagship destination. We also support applications to Canada and the Netherlands, and we advise on other countries on request — tell us where you want to go and we will assess your options honestly.
What English language qualification do I need?
Requirements vary by university and course — many UK institutions ask for IELTS (or an equivalent test), while some accept alternative evidence such as prior study in English. During your assessment we check the exact requirement for each course and advise on preparation options.
Can you help me find scholarships or funding?
We guide you through scholarships, bursaries and fee discounts offered by universities and external bodies, and help you present a strong application. We do not provide loans or sponsorships ourselves, and we will never promise funding that is not confirmed.
How early should I start my application?
Ideally 6–12 months before your intended intake. This leaves room for English tests, document gathering, offer conditions, visa processing and accommodation. Late applications can still succeed for some intakes — contact us and we will assess your timeline.
Do you support students after they arrive?
Yes. Our support does not end at the airport — student orientation covers enrolment, registration, banking, SIM cards and settling in, and our team stays reachable throughout your studies.
